# Telstra Plus × Dynamic Yield — Demo Flows

**One-line pitch:** *Dynamic Yield personalises every Telstra Plus screen — Home card, hub, Rewards Store, reward page, bag, earn and benefits — to each member's stage, balance and interests, while Shopping Muse (the "Telstra Plus assistant") turns an abstract points balance into attainable rewards through conversation.*

**Controls:** `P` or the **Presenter** button = presenter panel (switch member, persona story, jump to any screen, reset). Tap **Why this?** on a personalised card to open the **Why shown now** rail — it also lists the *live session signals* (category affinity) being built as you browse. The blue **chat bubble** inside Telstra Plus opens the assistant. Bottom nav: **Home** covers Telstra Plus hub / Earn / Benefits; **Shop** is the Rewards Store (store, reward page and bag highlight Shop).

**Run order:** Alex first (education-led, small balance) → Sarah (reactivation, big idle balance) → finish with a **live switch** on the Telstra Plus hub so the audience sees the same screen change completely.

## PERSONA 1 — Alex Nguyen · New Telstra Plus member
*Joined 11 days ago · one mobile plan · Member tier · 12,400 pts · never redeemed · **first visit to Telstra Plus, no browsing history**.*

**Objective:** educate a brand-new member on earn / use / Points + Pay, show **real-time personalisation** build up within a single session, make the first redemption feel achievable with a small balance, and set a next goal.

| # | Do this | Say this / DY capability |
|---|---------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Home** — point at "G'day, Alex", the **Member · 12,400 pts** tier card and the welcome card *"You've already earned 12,400 points"* | "DY recognises a new member with little history and leads with recognition and education, not promotion." → *new-member audience, points-balance personalisation* |
| 2 | Tap **Why this?** — note **Live session signals: Nothing yet** | "Every treatment is explainable — and right now DY knows almost nothing about Alex." → *Why shown now, cold start* |
| 3 | Tap the tier card → **Telstra Plus hub**. Show the education hero, the **Get set up** checklist (2 of 4) and the three **How Telstra Plus works** tiles | "Education-led onboarding in context. We can A/B this against a rewards-led variant." → *education vs reward-led onboarding test* |
| 4 | Scroll to **Popular rewards within your 12,400 points** — a deliberately *generic* set: Woolworths card, cinema tickets, charger, cable | "No affinity yet, so DY falls back to popularity, filtered to what Alex can actually get today. Remember these four." → *cold-start recs within balance* |
| 5 | Tap **Shop** in the bottom nav → **Rewards Store** (Shop tab now highlighted). Tap the **Home office** category, flick **Within my points** off, open **Logitech MX Keys S Keyboard** | "Alex is just browsing — one category tap and one product view. Each is a signal." → *behavioural signal capture* |
| 6 | Tap **Home** → tap the tier card → back on the **hub**. The section is now **"Picked for your home office · Updated just now from what you've browsed"**: Logitech desk mat, MX Anywhere mouse, Wave Keys, Brio webcam | "Same session, same screen, different recommendations — that's real-time personalisation from two taps, not a nightly batch." → *real-time affinity, in-session re-ranking* |
| 7 | Tap **Why this?** — **Live session signals** now shows *Home office* first | "And it's transparent: here's the signal that changed the page." → *Why shown now* |
| 8 | *(Optional)* Tap **Shop** again — **Home office** has moved to the front of the category chips | "The store re-orders itself around him too." → *category personalisation* |
| 9 | Tap the **chat bubble** → assistant. Tap **Show me how it works** (proactive nudge) | "The assistant teaches as well as sells — earn, use, Points + Pay — grounded in the knowledge article." → *Shopping Muse support + education* |
| 10 | Tap **Upgrade my home office** → **A better keyboard and mouse** | "Needs-based guided discovery: one light follow-up, then products from the live Rewards Store feed. This conversation also feeds his affinity." → *guided selling, conversational refinement* |
| 11 | Tap **Logitech MX Anywhere 3S Mouse** → **Reward page**. Show the **Use your Telstra Plus points** panel: slider defaults to all 12,400 pts (+ $108.97); drag it to show the live split; **Points + Pay** carries the **Recommended** badge; **Pay by card** shows he'd earn 1,390 pts | "He's 45,000 short, so DY recommends Points + Pay and lets him choose exactly how many points to use. Every cash dollar still earns 10 points." → *Points + Pay personalisation, earn-while-you-burn* |
| 12 | Scroll: **Complete your set-up** (cable, charger — all within points) | "Standard complementary recs stay cleaner than another conversation here." → *complete-the-set recs* |
| 13 | **Add to bag** → bag shows the per-item split and **A finishing touch** add-ons → **Redeem now → Confirm** | "First redemption done. Low-point add-ons with compatibility rules; no real payment." → *cart recs, low-point add-ons* |
| 14 | **You're all set** — pending points, "What should you save for next?" → **Find my next reward** | "The journey doesn't end at checkout — we immediately set the next earning goal." → *post-redemption next-best action* |
| 15 | In the assistant tap **How do I earn points faster?** → **See ways to earn** → on **Earn points** tap **Link a card for Shop and Earn** → **Link card** | "The checklist item completes; the earning loop is now wired up." → *earn education, card-link activation* |
| 16 | Optional: open any reward and tap **Keep earning toward this** — a progress bar appears on the hub and Earn screens | "A saved goal gives every future bill a purpose." → *goal setting, progress personalisation* |

**Close:** *"With zero history, DY taught Alex how the programme works, re-personalised his hub within the same session after two taps, got him to a first redemption with a points slider he controlled, and set up his next goal."*

> **Variation:** browse **Gaming** or **Headphones** instead of Home office in step 5 and the hub re-ranks to that category (Switch Online / PlayStation card / G305 / Astro headset, or JBL / Sony headphones). Press **Reset demo** in the presenter panel to clear the session signals and start cold again.

## PERSONA 2 — Sarah Mitchell · Gold member with idle points
*Gold for 6 years · family account (3 mobiles, nbn, Foxtel) · 186,500 pts, 22,000 expiring 31 Dec · no redemption in 14 months · linked Mastercard.*

**Objective:** reactivate a lapsed redeemer with expiry urgency, show high-value family rewards fully covered by points, promote Gold-only benefits and partner earning.

| # | Do this | Say this / DY capability |

| 2 | Tap **Why this?** | "Signals: expiring points, 14 months without a redemption, family household, Gold spend." → *Why shown now* |
| 3 | Tap the tier card → **Telstra Plus hub**. Expiry banner in the points band; hero *"186,500 points could get the whole family something"*; **Picked for your family, fully covered by points** (Ring doorbell, VTech KidiZoom, JBL Flip 7, Event Cinemas); **Your Gold benefits** | "Reactivation treatment: urgency + attainable, high-value family rewards + status reinforcement. No onboarding or education clutter." → *reactivation homepage, affinity recs, tier content* |

| 5 | **Chat bubble** → assistant → **Find rewards for my family** (proactive expiry nudge) | "The assistant opens with the business problem — expiring points — and answers with rewards from the feed." → *proactive engagement, balance-aware discovery* |
| 6 | Tap **Something for the kids** or type *"we are going on holiday soon"* | "Free-text intent → travel essentials within points, plus a nudge to earn 10 pts/$1 via TRAVLR." → *intent detection, earn + burn in one answer* |

## THE MONEY MOMENT — live switch
Stay on the **Telstra Plus hub** and press `P` → switch **Alex ↔ Sarah**. Hero, checklist/education, reward picks, assistant prompts and benefits teaser all change instantly. Use the **Persona story** card in the presenter panel to narrate signals, rationale and objectives.
